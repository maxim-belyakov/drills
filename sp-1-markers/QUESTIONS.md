# SP1 - the eight questions

Run after the technique block in DRILL.md. One question at a time. The rule: the first sentence must say how many parts the question has and which one the answer starts with. Starting without it is interrupted on the spot.

1. How do you check a new model version is fine?
2. How do you store state?
3. Tell me about your AI workflow
4. How is availability calculated?
5. How do you manage people?
6. What is your experience with Kubernetes?
7. How do you ensure code quality with AI?
8. What was the hardest part?

## 3. AI workflow - all four markers, first time
